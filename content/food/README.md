# Food data

Two layers, merged field by field at build time (`src/lib/food.ts`):

1. `ratings.json` — generated by `pnpm food:import <export.zip>`. Never hand-edit; the next import overwrites it.
   Scores, ranks, visits, photos and the original short notes all come from here.
2. `places/<slug>.md` — hand-written overlays, one per place, created with `pnpm food:edit <slug>`.
   Frontmatter: `id`, `status` (`draft` | `done` | `hidden`), optional `dishes`, `updated`. Body = the published note.
   - `draft`: the imported note shows, the body is ignored.
   - `done`: the body shows (empty body = no note).
   - `hidden`: no page, not in guides or the sitemap.
   Overlays win over `ratings.json` for the fields they carry. Scores can never be overridden.

`regions.json` groups cities into the areas the hub and guides are organised by. A city not listed here becomes
its own region automatically. Guides only exist for a region × category with at least 8 rated places.

A place gets its own page when it has at least one photo or a note; score-only places show up in guides as
unlinked rows. Photos are never committed: the import unpacks the export into `.food-export/` (gitignored).
